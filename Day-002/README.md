# Day 002 — Everything Is State

## Core Idea

Computers do not understand "studying", "sleeping", or "humans".

They only understand state.

Every operation is simply a transition from one state to another.

Before:

Energy = 100
Knowledge = 0

↓

Study()

↓

Energy = 85
Knowledge = 8

↓

Sleep()

↓

Energy = 105
Knowledge = 8

Programming is the art of designing valid state transitions.

This idea scales from variables, to objects, to operating systems, databases, distributed systems, and even civilizations.
